Early-access terms

Plain boundaries for the interest form, product previews, and any conversation that follows.

Effective July 24, 2026

Interest, not enrollment

Submitting the fit check asks Clearline Technology Methods to consider your church for a Pews early-access conversation. It does not guarantee acceptance, access, availability, timing, onboarding, support, or any particular feature.

No purchase through the fit check

No payment is collected through this page or the fit check. The published Founding Ops Beta price is $39 per month or $399 prepaid for one year, but submitting interest is not an order, enrollment, or promise to buy. Scope, start date, billing, data access, support, and exit terms will be agreed separately before participation begins.

Product previews

Interfaces and workflows shown on the Pews landing page illustrate product direction using sample data. They are not customer testimonials, customer results, live production data, or a representation that every depicted capability is production-ready.

Any beta will have separate boundaries

If your church is invited to evaluate Pews, we will agree on scope, permitted data, responsibilities, support, timing, billing, founding-rate protection, and exit or deletion steps before real use. Do not submit congregant records, giving records, pastoral-care details, credentials, or other sensitive church data through the interest form.

Feedback

You may share product and workflow feedback during the early-access process. We may use that feedback to improve Pews without owing compensation, but we will not identify your church publicly or use your name, logo, quote, or results as marketing proof without permission.

Availability and changes

The interest page and fit check are provided as available for evaluation and may change, pause, or close. These terms may also change as the program develops; the effective date above identifies the version that applies to this page.

Contact

Questions about the early-access process can be sent to intake@clearlinetechmethods.com. Personal-information requests are described in the privacy notice.

Short version: The page is an honest invitation to a fit conversation—not a production-readiness claim, purchase agreement, or request for sensitive church data.

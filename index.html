Questions

What churches usually ask first.

Is Pews replacing Planning Center?

No. Planning Center stays in place during this beta. Pews adds a weekly view and carries follow-up work beside it. Any future replacement decision would be made one workflow at a time.

What do we get for $39/month?

The Founding Ops Beta includes the weekly operations view, owners and due dates, guest and care follow-ups, service and volunteer-gap visibility, normal staff and volunteer access, and guided onboarding. The annual option is $399 prepaid.

What will we need to change?

Start with one real workflow and name one church-side owner. Your existing systems and source records stay in place. The exact connection work depends on your setup and available source access.

What is not part of the beta?

Full migration, donation processing, accounting, check-in hardware, native worship planning, mass messaging, and a finished member app are not included in this phase.

What happens after the fit check?

We review your answers. If the beta looks useful for both sides, we schedule a short conversation about one workflow, confirm the boundaries, and agree on scope, timing, billing, data access, and exit terms before anything begins.
